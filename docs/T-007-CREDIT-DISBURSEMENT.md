# T-007 (first cycle) — Credit Disbursement: the birth of the loan

Scope of this cycle: `ready_for_disbursement` formalization → disbursement command → money movement (Cash port) →
**ACTIVE loan** with its contractual obligations. Deferred to later cycles of the Loan Runtime package: payments,
payment applications, reversals, adjustments, collections/cross-branch payments, outbox, accounting entries.

`requested_amount != approved_amount != disbursed_amount`: this is the first package where `disbursed_amount` exists.
It is copied from the **confirmed cash movement**, never from the request; there is no amount field in the command.

## Flow (one transaction)

1. Lock the formalization row `FOR UPDATE` (tenant-filtered; foreign ids are 404).
2. Explicit `disbursement_branch_id` (same tenant, active) + `loans.disburse` permission **at that branch** (a separate
   permission from approve/formalize). The branch is never inferred from origin/managing.
3. Idempotency decided under the lock: same key + same content → replay; same key + other content → 409
   `idempotency_conflict`; other key + equivalent content → replay; other content on a disbursed contract → 409
   `already_disbursed`. `UNIQUE(formalization)`, `UNIQUE(tenant, idempotency_key)`, `UNIQUE(cash_movement)` are the backstop.
4. Contract integrity from the **frozen contract only** (`verify_formalization_contract`): contract hash, the embedded
   T-005 snapshot re-hashed from its own content, approved terms vs the approval row. A retired/inactive/superseded live
   product is NOT consulted (before formalization T-006 still blocks; after it, the contract stands).
5. The schedule is generated by the T-005 engine from the frozen snapshot rules, the approved amount/term and the
   business date in the **contract's timezone** (never the UTC date).
6. Cash port: locks box → session (`FOR UPDATE`), requires an open session of the disbursement branch's box, RD$, cents,
   enough cash; writes a `credit_disbursement` movement (a kind the legacy reversal does not accept) + cash audit.
7. Loan (`active`), disbursement (`confirmed`, `disbursed_amount` = confirmed movement), obligations, formalization
   `ready_for_disbursement → disbursed` (the only transition, enforced by trigger), audit `loan.disbursed` + `loan.activated`.
   Any exception rolls back ALL of it (cash included); a loser of a race consumes no loan number.

## Model (migration 0009)

`credit_loans` (original principal, frozen contract hashes, three explicit branches, composite tenant-safe FKs, immutable
except `status`), `credit_loan_disbursements` (append-only), `credit_loan_obligations` (contractual schedule, immutable except
`status`; `total = principal + interest + fees + delinquency`). Balances (`outstanding_principal`, `total_debt`) are DERIVED from the
obligations, never stored. 2 permissions: `loans.read`, `loans.disburse`.

## BLOCKED_BY_SPEC / BLOCKED_BY_EVIDENCE (nothing invented)

* Partial vs full disbursement and multiple tranches: only a full disbursement of the approved amount exists.
* Fees collected at disbursement (`at_origination`): a contract with such a fee is refused with 409
  `disbursement_blocked_by_spec`.
* Funding-source business rules; additional disbursement approval; disbursement amount limits; who may move money out of a
  specific cashier's session (any holder of `loans.disburse` at the branch, using an open session of that branch's box).
* Bank/provider-confirmed disbursement (no ledger or provider); non-DOP cash (the legacy ledger is RD$ only).
* Reversal/void of a disbursement, accounting policy, guarantors/guarantees, legal documents, `needs_information`.
